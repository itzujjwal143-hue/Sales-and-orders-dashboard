# 📊 Sales & Orders Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales & Orders Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, order trends, profitability, products, and regional performance.

The dashboard transforms raw sales and order data into meaningful business insights through interactive **KPIs, charts, filters, and visualizations**.

This project demonstrates practical skills in **data analysis, data visualization, KPI development, and dashboard design using Microsoft Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Aim

To develop an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides a clear overview of **sales, orders, profit, products, and regional performance** and helps users understand key business trends.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and order performance.
* Track important business KPIs such as **Total Profit**.
* Identify products and categories contributing to sales.
* Analyze sales performance across different regions and countries.
* Understand monthly sales trends.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing areas.
* Present business data through an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.
* Enable users to explore the data using filters and visual interactions.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis across multiple dimensions.

### 1. Profit Performance

Profit can be analyzed to understand the overall business performance and changes over time.

### 2. Profit by Product

The dashboard compares profit generated by different products, helping identify products that contribute significantly to overall revenue.

### 3. Profit by Region

Regional analysis provides a comparison of profit performance across different geographical areas.

### 4. Profit by Country

Country-level analysis helps identify geographical markets with different levels of sales contribution.

### 5. Monthly & Yearly Trends

Time-based visualizations help understand how sales change across months and years.

### 6. Profit Analysis

Profit-related KPIs and visualizations help evaluate whether sales are translating into positive business returns.
